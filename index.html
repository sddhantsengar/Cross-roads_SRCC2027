About Crossroads

Crossroads is the annual cultural festival of Shri Ram College of Commerce, University of Delhi. A celebration of talent, creativity, competition and entertainment, it brings together students, artists and institutions on a common platform.

As we prepare for Crossroads 2027, we invite enthusiastic and committed second-year students to take up leadership responsibilities as Senior Coordinators and contribute to making the festival a success.

The recruitment is organised across two major divisions:

Core TeamSponsorshipMarketing & MediaOutreachLogistics & Operations
Events TeamSponsorshipOutreach

Applicants can select their preferred division and indicate their departmental preferences in the application form.
