Crossroads 2027

Roles & Responsibilities

Core Team

1

Sponsorship

Lead sponsorship initiatives, identify potential sponsors, pitch proposals, negotiate partnerships and work towards achieving the assigned sponsorship targets. Guide junior team members and manage sponsor relationships and deliverables.

2

Marketing & Media

Promote Crossroads across Delhi University and beyond, maximise audience reach and drive participation. Handle promotional campaigns, college-level publicity, social media strategy, content creation and media coverage.

3

Outreach

Build connections with colleges, student societies, emerging artists, influencers and independent performers. Coordinate daytime performances, invite participating groups and ensure continuous engagement throughout the festival.

4

Logistics & Operations

Manage the operational backbone of Crossroads, including venue arrangements, infrastructure, transportation, accommodation, resource allocation, security coordination and on-ground execution. Ensure smooth coordination between departments.

General expectations from Senior Coordinators

  • Lead, guide and supervise Junior Coordinators.
  • Take ownership of departmental responsibilities and deliverables.
  • Maintain regular communication with Chief Coordinators.
  • Participate in weekly reviews and interdepartmental meetings.
  • Ensure timely planning and execution.
  • Collaborate across departments for the successful execution of Crossroads 2027.
